Mobile BCI for Game-based Motor Training and Neurorehabilitation

A volunteer wearing EMOTIV Flex 2 EEG headset controlling our mBCI maze game in iPad in real-time.

Mobile BCI with a low-latency closed loop between wearable EEG and a game engine. Built on AsyncDCNN, a coherence-based neuromorphic model with cross-session and cross-subject transfer for robust decoding of motor intent. AsyncDCNN introduces a coherence-based perceptron that models stable synaptic states with graded updates from pre- and post-synaptic activity. This supports asynchronous, parallel learning across forward, backward, and lateral pathways for reliable real-time decoding under non-stationary EEG.

SMT-Learner — Self-Supervised Foundation Model for Physiological Time-Series

A transformer auto-encoder trained with masked reconstruction on ~16,320 human and ~23,639 non-human-primate movement trajectories, learning transferable representations of motor behavior under sparse supervision. From these representations I derive a novel Exploration–Exploitation biomarker that separates early- from late-phase motor skill learning — a construct long theorized in motor neuroscience but difficult to measure outside a laboratory.

Key contributions
  • Masked-reconstruction pretraining over spatiotemporal movement trajectories.
  • Evaluation via frozen-encoder linear probing, cross-cohort and cross-species transfer, and systematic ablations on masking ratio and cohort size.
  • Cross-species transfer as evidence that representations capture motor-control structure rather than cohort-specific artifacts.
