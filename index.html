Hi there!

I’m a final-year Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at Marquette University (defending Fall 2026), advised by Dr. Sheikh Iqbal Ahamed and co-advised by Dr. Samuel Nemanich, and Founding Research Director at Ubicomply.ai, where I lead HPC and agentic-AI engineering teams. My work spans self-supervised foundation models for physiological time-series, edge AI and mobile brain–computer interfaces, agentic AI for regulated domains, and large-scale healthcare data harmonization — built end to end, from raw sensor data to systems clinicians and customers actually use.

Dissertation: Digital health platforms and machine learning for pediatric motor control and performance
Integrated mHealth and real-time AI systems with multimodal biosensors to quantify motor learning and enable motor training for neurorehabilitation.
Supported by: NIH (R03HD107651, R21HD114094) · Northwestern C-STAR · 2026 NMDSI Paving ROADS Seed Award

Background: Originally from Dhaka, Bangladesh, I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BUET. Over 6+ years I have built large-scale health informatics and ML systems in both research and commercial settings — including a privacy-preserving national clinical data warehouse integrating de-identified records from 220 healthcare systems (Elsevier Smart Health Best Paper Award). I have 17 peer-reviewed publications and contributed technical aims to $2.65M+ in awarded funding. I am a U.S. Permanent Resident (National Interest Waiver).

Open to Research Scientist, Applied/ML Scientist, and AI Engineering roles in industry or research institutions.
